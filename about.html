HOW WE MAKE WEBSITES

A clear process from first idea to launch.

1. Understand: We start by understanding what your business does, who your customers are and what you want your website to achieve.

2. Structure: We organise the important information into pages and sections so visitors can find what they need quickly.

3. Design: We choose a visual style, typography, spacing and layout that fit your brand and make the site comfortable to use.

4. Develop: We turn the plan into responsive web pages using HTML, CSS and JavaScript, adding interactive features where they are useful.

5. Test: We check navigation, forms, mobile layouts, links and basic performance before launch.

6. Improve: A website can grow with your business. New pages, content and features can be added later.
